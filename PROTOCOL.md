# Public Protocol --- v0.1

This is a minimum launch protocol, not a final constitution or governance system.

1. **No impersonation or unauthorized representation.** Do not present an invented, simulated, summarized, or inferred statement as if it were the speaker's own statement.

2. **Preserve provenance.** Where relevant, distinguish Human / AI-assisted / AI-generated / AI-inferred. If AI adds a subject, object, intention, relationship, certainty, attitude, or other meaning not explicit in the source, that intervention should be identifiable.

3. **Keep identity-disclosure criteria internal.** The conditions, thresholds, and procedures for any future disclosure of the anonymous proposer's identity are not part of this public launch protocol. Their non-public status does not authorize false statements about identity or provenance.

4. **Separate Privacy Masking from Editorial Editing.** Removing information to reduce re-identification risk is different from rewriting for clarity, brevity, translation, or style.

5. **Preserve unresolved matters and dissent.** Do not convert disagreement, uncertainty, abstention, unanswered questions, or minority positions into consensus.

6. **Maintain reversibility to source.** AI summaries, translations, interpretations, and derived claims should, where practicable, retain a path back to the specific source version from which they were produced. Long-term resolvability matters.

7. **Preserve meaningful ambiguity.** Do not silently convert ambiguity, hesitation, omitted subjects, uncertainty, or culturally specific expression into a more definite position merely to make language sound natural. If interpretation is necessary, disclose it.

8. **Truthfulness does not require full disclosure.** Information may be withheld for privacy, anonymity, confidentiality, or safety. Withholding does not authorize fabrication.

These rules are intentionally minimal. They may be challenged, amended, or supplemented in the Living Version without rewriting the frozen launch record.
